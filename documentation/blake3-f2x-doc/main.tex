\vspace{0.5em}

\begin{abstract}
We describe a Blake3 arithmetisation that commits all witness columns
as bit-polynomials in $\F_2[X]$. The design rests on three
$\F_2[X]$-native primitives, all inherited verbatim from
\texttt{sha-f2x-doc}: \emph{(i)}~$\XOR$ is polynomial addition in
$\F_2[X]$ and is free at the commitment layer; \emph{(ii)}~the four
fixed rotations $\ROTR^{16}, \ROTR^{12}, \ROTR^{8}, \ROTR^{7}$ that
Blake3's $\Gmix$ mixing function uses are multiplications by the
constant monomials $X^{16}, X^{20}, X^{24}, X^{25}$ in
$\F_2[X]/(X^{32}-1)$; \emph{(iii)}~modular addition $\pmod{2^{32}}$ is
arithmetised by the per-step Binius identity with virtual carry in
$\F_2[X]/(X^{32})$, exactly as in \texttt{sha-f2x-doc}
(\Cref{F2:lem:binius-vc}): each binary step commits one $\F_2$ carry-out
bit and enforces one column-level Hadamard relation in $\Fshift$
together with one $\F_2$-linear LSB compensator equation. Two-input
sums use one binary step; three-input sums chain two binary steps with
one committed intermediate-sum bit-poly column.

In contrast to SHA-256, Blake3 has \emph{no} $\AND, \Ch, \Maj, \SHR$ or
$\sigma_i, \Sigma_i$ operations and \emph{no} modular-addition
feed-forward (Blake3's chaining-value feed-forward is $\XOR$, not
modular addition). Consequently the present arithmetisation requires
\emph{zero} non-addition Hadamards (vs.\ $3$ in
\texttt{sha-f2x-doc}), \emph{zero} dedicated rotation/shift-result
columns (vs.\ $4$), and \emph{zero} feed-forward Binius steps (vs.\
$2$). The Hadamard relations that remain are all addition-side and
are listed explicitly in \Cref{sec:uair}. The result is a constraint
system whose final state is a single MLE-evaluation claim per
committed column over the verifier's challenge extension field, with
tight local soundness on every modular addition.
\end{abstract}
