\section{Soundness and Fiat--Shamir}
\label{sec:soundness}

We assemble the per-stage guarantees of
\Cref{sec:arith,sec:piop,sec:hadamard,sec:commitment} into a single statement,
then note its compilation to a non-interactive SNARK. The treatment is at the
level of error accounting and composition; each ingredient's bound was
established in its own section.

\subsection{The end-to-end guarantee}
\label{sec:e2e}

\begin{theorem}[Knowledge soundness, informal]
\label{thm:e2e}
The interactive argument of \Cref{sec:piop,sec:hadamard,sec:commitment} is a
knowledge-sound argument for the SHA-256 chained-compression relation: there is
an extractor that, from any prover making the verifier accept with
non-negligible probability, recovers a satisfying SHA-256 witness (the message
blocks chaining $H_0$ to the public digest $H_N$), except with probability at
most
\[
  \epsilon \;\le\;
  \epsilon_{\mathrm{prox}}(t,\delta)
  \;+\;
  O\!\left(\frac{N_{\mathrm{con}} \cdot \wbit + K_H + \nu}{|\K|}\right),
\]
where $\epsilon_{\mathrm{prox}}(t,\delta)$ is the commitment code's $t$-query
proximity error, $N_{\mathrm{con}}$ the number of constraint families, $K_H$ the
number of Hadamard relations, and $\nu$ the trace $\log$-height. The first term
dominates.
\end{theorem}

The rest of this section sketches the proof: the arithmetization is an exact
reduction, each protocol stage contributes a bounded error, and the stages
compose round-by-round.

\subsection{Arithmetization equivalence}
\label{sec:arith-equiv}

By construction (\Cref{sec:arith}), the UAIR is satisfiable by a trace $T$ iff
there is a SHA-256 witness for the public $(H_0,H_N,\text{message})$: each linear
identity and each Hadamard relation is, under the integer reading $\psi_2$,
equivalent to the corresponding SHA-256 step
(Lemmas~\ref{lem:rot},~\ref{lem:binius},~\ref{lem:and}), and the boundary
constraints pin the chaining and the public digest. So extracting a satisfying
trace is the same as extracting a SHA-256 witness; this step contributes no
error.

\subsection{Per-stage errors}
\label{sec:per-stage}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item \emph{Linear path}
    (\Cref{sec:ideal-check,sec:psi-alpha,sec:sumcheck}). The ideal check's
    random $\rho$, the reading $\psia$, and the degree-$2$ sumcheck each
    contribute a Schwartz--Zippel term. A false ideal-membership that survives
    $\psia$ must have its degree-$<\wbit$ cell polynomial vanish at $\alpha$
    ($\le(\wbit-1)/|\K|$ per family, by \Cref{lem:linear-survives}); $\rho$ and
    the sumcheck add $O(\nu/|\K|)$. Total
    $O(N_{\mathrm{con}}\wbit/|\K| + \nu/|\K|)$.
  \item \emph{Hadamard discharge} (\Cref{lem:psi-z-sound}). The
    $\langle Z_H\rangle$ check at $\alpha$, the relation batch, and the shared
    row sumcheck contribute $\frac{2(\wbit-1)+K_H+\nu}{|\K|}$.
  \item \emph{Multipoint evaluation} (\Cref{sec:multipoint}): a sumcheck reducing
    the claims at $r^*$ and the pointed shifts to $r_0$, $O(\nu/|\K|)$.
  \item \emph{Opening} (\Cref{sec:pcs-checks}):
    $\epsilon_{\mathrm{open}}\le\epsilon_{\mathrm{prox}}(t,\delta)+O(1/|\K|)$,
    the proximity term dominating.
  \item \emph{Binding} (\Cref{eq:psi-z-binding}): the single $\psiz$ check on the
    bound $a'$, $O(1/|\K|)$.
\end{itemize}
Summing gives the bound of \Cref{thm:e2e}.

\subsection{Composition and extraction}
\label{sec:composition}

Each stage is a public-coin reduction that is \emph{round-by-round knowledge
sound} (RBR-KS): on a ``doomed'' transcript prefix (no witness can explain it),
the next challenge keeps it doomed except with the stage's error. The
Zinc\texttt{+} compiler is designed so that composing RBR-KS reductions preserves
RBR-KS, with errors adding. Hence the whole chain --- commit, ideal check,
$\psia$, sumcheck (carrying the Hadamard group), multipoint eval, open --- is
RBR-KS with the summed error of \Cref{sec:per-stage}.

The extractor runs bottom-up. The opening's extractor (companion note) recovers
committed columns $\widehat w_1,\dots,\widehat w_K$. On an accepting run, the
opening certifies that the $\psia$- and $\psiz$-claims at $r_0$ are the true
projected evaluations of the $\widehat w_g$ (\Cref{sec:pcs-certifies}); the
linear-path and Hadamard soundness then certify that the $\widehat w_g$ satisfy
every UAIR constraint; and arithmetization equivalence yields the SHA-256
witness. No rewinding beyond the opening extractor is required, because every
PIOP message is a value of a polynomial identity or a pre-committed datum, not a
fresh commitment.

\subsection{The non-interactive argument}
\label{sec:fiat-shamir}

The argument is public-coin, and every challenge is drawn from a transcript into
which the data it tests has already been absorbed. It therefore compiles, under
the Fiat--Shamir / BCS transform in the random-oracle model, to a non-interactive
argument of knowledge with the same error up to the standard ROM loss (quadratic
in the query budget). Instantiated with the codebase's hash and parameters, this
is the deployed SNARK.

\subsection{Caveats}
\label{sec:caveats}

Three honest qualifications, consistent with the construction's current state.

\paragraph{The adder-carry binding.} As flagged in \Cref{rem:adder-trusted}, the
Binius adder operands' $\psiz$ parents are supplied trusted rather than bound to
the commitment. \Cref{thm:e2e} is therefore unconditional for the $\AND$
relations and the linear part, but for the modular-addition Hadamards it holds
assuming honest adder parents. Committing the carry-out bit and giving the carry
a sound row/bit-shift binding closes this to an unconditional statement; it is a
localized, recorded follow-up that does not affect the rest of the composition.

\paragraph{Not zero-knowledge.} The construction proves knowledge but is not
zero-knowledge: the opening reveals committed cells at the queried positions, and
no masking is applied. Adding zero-knowledge (masking the codeword and the
sumcheck) is future work, orthogonal to the structure here.

\paragraph{Proof size.} As a Brakedown/Ligero-style IOPP, the proof is large ---
the $t$ column openings of \Cref{sec:pcs-checks} dominate it --- and grows with
the trace. The construction optimizes prover time and the linear/Hadamard
sharing, not proof length; a recursive (WHIR-style) IOPP in place of the flat
proximity test would shrink it and is independent of everything above
(\Cref{rem:blaze}).
