\section{The Deployed Instantiation}
\label{sec:impl}

The construction is realised in the Zinc\texttt{+} reference codebase on the
\texttt{f2-clean} branch. This section fixes the generic parameters of the
preceding sections to their deployed values, records the concrete trace
shape, and reports the measurements that motivated the design --- above all
the oblong Hadamard discharge of \Cref{sec:hadamard}, which is the part of
the prover the instantiation most aggressively optimizes.

The field width $\kappa=128$ (rather than a wider binary field) sets the
soundness denominator $2^{128}$ and was chosen to minimise the carryless
multiplication count; it enters \Cref{thm:e2e} only through that
denominator. The trace height is a free parameter: $N$ chained compressions
fit in $\approx 68N$ rows, so a taller trace proves a longer hash at the
same per-row cost, and the benchmarks sweep $\nu\in\{9,14,16,19,20,21,22\}$.

\subsection{Trace shape}
\label{sec:impl-trace}

At the smallest height ($\nu=9$, i.e.\ $512$ rows) the trace holds seven
chained SHA-256 compressions. The column inventory realises the inventory
of \Cref{sec:air}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item \emph{Public} (not committed): round constants, message words, the
    packed LSB-compensator column, and a few $\{0,1\}$ selectors --- about
    eight columns.
  \item \emph{Primary witness} (committed): the state columns
    $\col{A},\col{E},\col{W}$, the four mixing outputs, the three $\AND$
    product columns, the round intermediates, and the chained-addition
    intermediate sums --- about twenty columns.
  \item \emph{Virtual} (reconstructed): the $\SHR$ extractions used by
    $\sigma_0,\sigma_1$ and the per-step compensator --- about twelve
    columns, none committed.
\end{itemize}
Committed columns are stored two trace cells to a $64$-bit word (the
cell-pairing optimization of the companion note), so the Merkle tree
carries roughly ten packed columns. Every constraint of the running SHA-256 is one
of the four families of \Cref{sec:air}, and the entire nonlinear content is
the $K_H=16$ Hadamard relations of \Cref{sec:had-oblig}.

\subsection{The Hadamard discharge, measured}
\label{sec:impl-discharge}

The empirical centre of gravity is the Hadamard discharge. In the
fused-projection predecessor it consumed the overwhelming majority of the
prover --- about $92\%$ at $\nu=16$ --- because it ran the bit-slice
sumcheck for all $K_H\wbit$ bit equations in the full field $\K$. The
oblong argument of \Cref{sec:hadamard} keeps the base-field witness in the
base field, and the GF$(2^8)$ variant (\Cref{rem:gf8}) pushes the
accumulation into an embedded byte field. The standalone discharge
A/B (Apple~M4, \texttt{parallel}\,$+$\,\texttt{simd}, medians, thermal-dependent):

Wired end-to-end with the sound $\psiz$ binding of \Cref{sec:had-binding},
the picture at $\nu=16$ is: the linear path alone (commit, ideal check,
$\psia$, sumcheck, open, no discharge) proves in $\approx 46$ ms; the full
prove with the \emph{fused} discharge in $\approx 487$ ms; and the full
prove with the \emph{oblong} discharge in $\approx 136$ ms --- a
$\sim\!3.6\times$ faster Hadamard prove, with the binding adding only a few
milliseconds on top of the discharge. The verifier is essentially
unchanged across the two discharges, $\approx 11$ ms at $\nu=16$, dominated
by the $t$ column-opening proximity checks. These are representative
medians on one machine; the \emph{ratios} are more stable than the
absolute figures, which drift with thermal state.

\begin{remark}[Prover cost structure]
\label{rem:cost-structure}
With the oblong discharge in place, the prover's remaining time is spread
across the commit (encode $+$ Merkle), the linear PIOP (ideal check,
projection, sumcheck), the discharge, and the opening. The dominant
\emph{verifier} term, and the dominant \emph{proof-size} term, is the
column-opening proximity test of \Cref{sec:pcs-checks}; the algebraic
messages are comparatively small.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Caveats of the instantiation}
\label{sec:impl-caveats}

The qualifications of \Cref{sec:caveats} are concrete here. The
modular-addition Hadamards' $\psiz$ parents are supplied trusted
(\Cref{rem:adder-trusted}), so the deployed prover is at fused-discharge
soundness parity for the adders; the $\AND$ relations and the entire linear
part are unconditional. The proof is not zero-knowledge and is large in the
Brakedown sense, the column openings dominating. Some components were
machine-assisted and are under author review. None of these touch the
structural claims of \Cref{sec:arith,sec:piop,sec:hadamard}.

\subsection{Outlook}
\label{sec:outlook}

The all-$\Ftwo[X]$ SHA-256 argument shows what a polynomial-ring
arithmetization buys for a bit-oriented computation. Keeping words as
bit-polynomials makes $\XOR$ free, shifts and rotations virtual, and
modular addition a one-bit-per-add Binius identity, leaving the single
nonlinear primitive $\AND$ as column Hadamard products. The proof system
then organizes itself around the linear/Hadamard seam: a binary-field PIOP
discharges the linear constraints through the evaluation projection
$\psia$; an oblong zerocheck discharges the Hadamard products through a
second projection $\psiz$; and one bit-sliced commitment opening binds the
full bit-slice fold, serving both projections at once. The recurring
mechanism is the $\Ftwo$-linearity of the cell projections and the code,
which lets random projections commute through the linear machinery and lets
a single committed object answer many functionals.

Three directions remain. A sound binding for the adder carries would make
\Cref{thm:e2e} unconditional. A recursive (WHIR-style) proximity test in
place of the flat one would shrink the proof. And the verifier-visible
word-level reshaping of the Hadamard discharge --- dropping the
$\times\wbit$ bit-slice factor entirely, in the manner of Binius --- would
compound the speedups reported above. None disturbs the arithmetization or
the linear/Hadamard architecture; each is a localized improvement to a
layer whose interface to the others is exactly the projected evaluation
claims this paper has traced from SHA-256 down to the commitment.
