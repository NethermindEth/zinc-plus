\section{The Deployed Instantiation}
\label{sec:impl}

The construction is realised in the Zinc\texttt{+} reference codebase on the
\texttt{f2-clean} branch. This section fixes the generic parameters of the
preceding sections to their deployed values, records the trace shape, and
reports the headline measurements. The full scaling sweeps and the per-phase
decomposition are deferred to \Cref{app:meas}.

The field width $\kappa = 128$ sets the soundness denominator $2^{128}$ and was
chosen to minimise the carryless-multiplication count; it enters \Cref{thm:e2e}
only through that denominator. The trace height is free: $N$ chained
compressions fit in $\approx 68N$ rows, so a taller trace proves a longer hash at
the same per-row cost, and the benchmarks sweep
$\nu\in\{9,14,16,19,20,21,22\}$.

\subsection{Trace shape}
\label{sec:impl-trace}

At the smallest height ($\nu = 9$, i.e.\ $512$ rows) the trace holds seven
chained compressions, realising the inventory of \Cref{sec:air}: about eight
\emph{public} columns (round constants, message words, the LSB-compensator
column, selectors); about twelve \emph{primary witness} columns
($\col{A},\col{E},\col{W}$, the two $\AND$ products, the round intermediates, the
chained-addition partial sums); and the \emph{virtual} columns (the four
$\Sigma/\sigma$ mixing outputs, the $\SHR^{1}$ carry words, the compensator),
none committed. Committed columns are stored two trace cells to a $64$-bit word
(the cell-pairing optimization of the companion note), so the Merkle tree carries
roughly six packed columns. The entire nonlinear content is the $K_H = 14$
Hadamard relations.

\subsection{The prover, measured}
\label{sec:impl-discharge}

The prover's natural centre of gravity is the Hadamard discharge: it carries the
\emph{only} nonlinearity, $K_H\wbit$ bit-products, where the linear part is one
pass of $\Ftwo$-linear algebra. The design of \Cref{sec:hadamard} keeps that cost
small --- the bit dimension is folded once at $\alpha$ (never a sumcheck
variable), the base-domain accumulation stays in the base field
(\Cref{rem:gf8}), and the discharge rides the linear part's ideal check and
sumcheck rather than running its own. Wired end-to-end with the binding of
\Cref{sec:had-binding}, the picture at $\nu = 16$ (Apple~M4,
\texttt{parallel}\,$+$\,\texttt{simd}, CPU, medians) is:

\begin{remark}[Prover cost structure]
\label{rem:cost-structure}
With the discharge in place, the prover's time is spread across the commit
(encode $+$ Merkle), the linear PIOP, the discharge, the multipoint eval, and the
opening (\Cref{tab:perphase}). The dominant \emph{verifier} term, and the
dominant \emph{proof-size} term, is the column-opening proximity test
(\Cref{sec:pcs-checks}); the algebraic messages are comparatively small. The
optional Metal GPU commit offloads the encode/Merkle phase but shows no net
prover-latency win at these scales once thermal state is matched --- its value is
throughput and freeing the CPU.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Prover kernels}
\label{sec:impl-kernels}

The two reductions after the commit --- the Hadamard sumcheck and the multipoint
evaluation --- are computed by kernels that change only the memory-access
pattern, not the messages, so the emitted proof is byte-identical to the naive
evaluation: the sumcheck streams the operands of one relation at a time in
coefficient form, and the multipoint evaluation builds its per-column selector
polynomials in parallel. Both are pure prover speed-ups that leave the soundness
analysis of \Cref{sec:soundness} untouched.

\subsection{Caveats of the instantiation}
\label{sec:impl-caveats}

The qualifications of \Cref{sec:caveats} are concrete here. The modular-addition
Hadamards' $\psiz$ parents are supplied trusted (\Cref{rem:adder-trusted}), so the
deployed prover is unconditional for the $\AND$ relations and the entire linear
part but assumes honest adder parents for the $12$ carry Hadamards. The proof is
not zero-knowledge and is large in the Brakedown sense, the column openings
dominating. Some components were machine-assisted and are under author review.
None of these touch the structural claims of
\Cref{sec:arith,sec:piop,sec:hadamard}.

\subsection{Outlook}
\label{sec:outlook}

Three directions remain, each localized and each leaving the arithmetization and
the linear/Hadamard architecture intact: a sound binding for the adder carries
would make \Cref{thm:e2e} unconditional (\Cref{rem:adder-trusted}); a recursive
(WHIR-style) proximity test would shrink the proof (\Cref{rem:blaze}); and a
word-level reshaping of the discharge --- dropping the $\times\wbit$ bit-slice
factor in the manner of Binius --- would compound the prover speedups.
