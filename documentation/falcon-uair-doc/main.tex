\begin{abstract}
We describe the Universal AIR with lookups (UAIR$^+$) instance that
arithmetizes the algebraic core of \textbf{Falcon} (FN-DSA, FIPS~206)
signature verification, as deployed in the Zinc+ reference
implementation. We verify a \emph{batch} of $2^{\nu}$ signatures
\emph{at once} under a shared public key, one signature per trace row.
We follow the conventions and notation of the main Zinc+ paper and
focus on the design choices specific to lattice-signature verification:
\emph{(i)}~a direct $\Z[X]$ ($+$~explicit mod-$q$ quotient)
arithmetization of the negacyclic ring relation
$s_1 + s_2 h \equiv c \pmod{q, X^n+1}$, expressed against the rotation
(negacyclic) ideal $\negacyc$; \emph{(ii)}~a \emph{product witness}
$s_3 = s_2 h$ that keeps the ring equation algebraically \emph{linear}
in the trace MLEs (so the instance is MLE-first eligible), with the
quadratic product relation discharged by a zero-ideal constraint bound
in the inner sumcheck; \emph{(iii)}~a direct sum-of-squares
arithmetization of the Euclidean-norm acceptance bound
$\lVert (s_1,s_2)\rVert^2 \le \nbound$ as a booleanity-adapted zerocheck
over the signature rows, reading the coefficient slices of the committed
limb cells; and \emph{(iv)}~a polynomial-commitment optimization that
folds the arbitrary-poly lane along the cell-degree axis, shrinking the
proof roughly $3.6\times$. $\HashToPoint$ (SHAKE-256) and $\Decompress$
are out of scope: the hash point $c$ is taken as public input and the
short vector $s_2$ as a witness.
\end{abstract}
