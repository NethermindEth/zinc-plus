% ===========================================================================
\section{Falcon overview and target relation}\label{sec:overview}
% ===========================================================================

We briefly recall Falcon to fix notation, then state the relation our
UAIR$^+$ arithmetizes.

\paragraph{The ring.}
Fix the prime $q = 12289$ and the degree $n = 512$ (Falcon-512; the
$n=1024$ variant is identical up to parameters). Let
\[
  \Rring := \Z[X]/(X^n+1), \qquad \Rq := \Z_q[X]/(X^n+1).
\]
$\Rring$ is the \emph{negacyclic} ring: multiplication reduces modulo
$X^n+1$, so $X^n \equiv -1$ and a wrap past degree $n$ flips sign. We
represent ring elements by their \emph{centered} coefficient vectors,
each coefficient in $(-\half, \half\,]$ with $\half = 6144$. The
acceptance bound is $\nbound = 34{,}034{,}726$ for Falcon-512.

\paragraph{Verification.}
A Falcon public key is $h \in \Rq$. A signature on a message $m$ (with
salt) consists of a short vector recovered by decompression as
$s_2 \in \Rring$; the verifier computes the hash point
$c = \HashToPoint(\text{salt}\,\Vert\,m) \in \Rq$, recovers the second
half
\begin{equation}\label{eq:s1}
  s_1 \;\equiv\; c - s_2\, h \pmod{q}, \qquad
  s_1 \in (-\half, \half\,]^n \text{ (centered)},
\end{equation}
and \emph{accepts} iff the Euclidean norm bound holds:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:norm}
  \lVert (s_1, s_2) \rVert^2
  \;=\; \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} s_1[i]^2 + \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} s_2[i]^2
  \;\le\; \nbound .
\end{equation}
Equivalently to \cref{eq:s1}, the pair $(s_1, s_2)$ satisfies the
\emph{ring equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ring}
  s_1 + s_2\, h \;\equiv\; c \pmod{q}\ \text{in } \Rq .
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Scope.}
We arithmetize the \emph{algebraic core}:
\cref{eq:ring} and \cref{eq:norm}. The two hashing steps are out of
scope, mirroring how the Zinc+ SHA-256$+$ECDSA demonstration covers the
multi-scalar-multiplication bulk rather than every mod-$n$ reduction:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item $\HashToPoint$ (a SHAKE-256 expansion mod $q$) is \emph{not}
  arithmetized; the hash point $c$ is supplied as a \emph{public}
  input. A verifier who recomputes $c$ in the clear can bind it to the
  trace.
  \item $\Decompress$ (the variable-length signature encoding) is
  \emph{not} arithmetized; $s_2$ is supplied as a \emph{witness}, and
  $s_1$ is supplied as a witness recomputed by the (honest) prover.
\end{itemize}
Both are standard, hash-/encoding-only steps that compose with the core
relation; we focus on the lattice arithmetic, which is where the Zinc+
ring machinery (ideal-membership constraints over $\Rring$, bit/coefficient
slicing) earns its keep.

\paragraph{Batching.}
Real deployments verify \emph{many} signatures under one key (e.g.\ a
validator checking a block of attestations). We therefore arithmetize a
\emph{batch} of $2^{\nu}$ signatures under a single shared public key
$h$, with a per-signature hash point $c$. Each signature occupies one
row of a $2^{\nu}$-row trace, so a single per-row constraint discharges
all $2^{\nu}$ ring equations and a single per-row zerocheck discharges
all $2^{\nu}$ norm bounds.
