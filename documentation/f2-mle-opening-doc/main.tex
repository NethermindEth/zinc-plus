\begin{abstract}
We describe the final step of the all-$\F_2$ variant of the Zinc+
PIOP: discharging the multilinear-extension evaluation claims that
the protocol's sumcheck emits over the binary field $\GFt$. The claims
have the form $\MLE{[\psia(v)]}(r^*) = a$ with
$r^* \in \GFt^{\nu}$, $a \in \GFt$, and $v$ a committed witness
column over the cell ring $\cellring = \F_2[X]/(X^{32})$. Naively
adapting the integer-typed Zip+ opening fails: the integer-side
lift exploits the ring homomorphism $\Z \to \F_q$, which has no
counterpart for $\F_2[X] \to \GFt$ at a generic $\alpha$. Our
construction instead lifts the row-folded MLE values to (unreduced)
polynomials in $\F_2[X]$, runs the Zip+ proximity check entirely
in $\F_2[X]$-arithmetic against the existing $\F_2$-RAA codeword
matrix, and discharges each per-column claim by one local
evaluation at $\alpha$. The lift is the unique inverse of the
$\F_2$-linear map $c \mapsto \sum_i c_i \alpha^i$, computed once per
$\alpha$ via a $192\times 192$ matrix inversion over $\F_2$. A
$\gamma$-batching step folds the per-column claims into a single
$\F_2[X]$-opening triple, leaving the proof linear in the
column-opening parameter and \emph{constant} in the number of
committed columns. We give the construction, sketch its soundness,
and report concrete proof-size accounting.
\end{abstract}
