\section{Completeness and Soundness}
\label{sec:soundness}

We first verify completeness, then analyse knowledge soundness by
walking the four checks backward from the commitment to the claim. The
analysis isolates the opening; we treat the upstream reduction's
knowledge-soundness error as a black box $\epsilon_{\mathrm{pre}}$ and
add only the opening's error.

\subsection{Completeness}

\begin{theorem}[Completeness]
\label{thm:completeness}
For an honest prover holding a witness whose commitment was produced by
\Cref{cons:commit} and whose claims satisfy
$a_g = \mle{\psia(w_g)}(r^*)$, the verifier of \Cref{prot:open} accepts
with probability $1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We verify each check on honest data, using $\K$-linearity of the maps
involved.

\emph{\ref{chk:eval}.} By \eqref{eq:b-batch} and \eqref{eq:a-batch},
\[
  \sum_i q_0[i]\,b'[i]
  = \sum_i q_0[i]\sum_g \gamma_g\,b_g[i]
  = \sum_g \gamma_g \sum_i q_0[i]\,b_g[i]
  = \sum_g \gamma_g\,a_g' = a'.
\]

\emph{\ref{chk:discharge}.} Since $\psia$ is $\K$-linear on $\Kpoly$
(\Cref{rem:psi-extends}) and $a' = \sum_g \gamma_g a_g'$,
\[
  \psia(a') = \sum_g \gamma_g\,\psia(a_g') = \sum_g \gamma_g\,a_g,
\]
where the last step is \Cref{lem:bitslice}.

\emph{\ref{chk:coherence}.} Substituting \eqref{eq:combined-row} and reordering,
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \sum_j q_1[j]\,\mathrm{cr}'[j]
  &= \sum_j q_1[j]\sum_g \gamma_g \sum_i c_i\,M_{w_g}[i,j] \\
  &= \sum_i c_i \sum_g \gamma_g \sum_j q_1[j]\,M_{w_g}[i,j]
   = \sum_i c_i \sum_g \gamma_g\,b_g[i]
   = \sum_i c_i\,b'[i].
\end{aligned}
\]

\emph{\ref{chk:encoding}.} For honest data $\mat{C}_g[i,\cdot] = \enc(M_{w_g}[i,\cdot])$.
By the scalar-extension property (\Cref{lem:linearity}),
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \enc(\mathrm{cr}')[j]
  &= \enc\!\Big(\sum_g \gamma_g \sum_i c_i\,M_{w_g}[i,\cdot]\Big)[j] \\
  &= \sum_g \gamma_g \sum_i c_i\,\enc(M_{w_g}[i,\cdot])[j] \\
  &= \sum_i c_i \sum_g \gamma_g\,\mat{C}_g[i,j],
\end{aligned}
\]
for every $j$, in particular at the opened $j_k$; and the honest Merkle
paths verify. All four checks pass.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[No abort event]
Unlike the $\alpha$-lift predecessor --- whose lift table fails to exist
when $\alpha$ falls in a proper subfield of $\K$, forcing a resample with
probability $\le 2^{-95}$ --- the bit-sliced open has \emph{no} such
event: it never inverts a matrix and never requires $\{1,\alpha,\dots\}$
to be a basis. Completeness is unconditional in $\alpha$.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Knowledge soundness}

We argue that an accepting transcript forces the claims to equal the true
evaluations of an extractable committed witness, up to a small additive
error. The argument is the standard Brakedown/Ligero backbone --- a
proximity test plus a consistency test --- specialised to the
$\gamma$-batched, bit-sliced setting.

\paragraph{Setup.} Fix a (possibly malicious) prover that produces an
accepting transcript with commitment $\mathrm{root}$ and proof
$(a', b', \mathrm{cr}', \{(j_k,\text{cells}_k,\pi_k)\})$. Let $\delta$ be
the relative minimum distance of the code and write
$\epsilon_{\mathrm{prox}}(t,\delta)$ for the proximity error of the
$t$-query column test --- $\bigl((1+\delta)/2\bigr)^t$ in the
unique-decoding regime, or the correlated-agreement (MCA) bound in the
list-decoding regime that the codebase targets. The deployed
$t = 987$ at rate $1/4$ places this term below $2^{-100}$
(\Cref{rem:distance}).

\begin{theorem}[Knowledge soundness of the opening]
\label{thm:soundness}
There is an extractor that, given an accepting transcript, outputs
committed messages $\widehat w_1,\dots,\widehat w_K \in \cellring^{\,n}$
consistent with $\mathrm{root}$ such that, except with probability at most
\[
  \epsilon_{\mathrm{open}}
  \;\le\;
  \epsilon_{\mathrm{prox}}(t,\delta)
  \;+\;
  \frac{1}{2^{\Kbits}}
  \;+\;
  \frac{1}{2^{\Kbits}},
\]
each accepted claim equals the true evaluation of the committed witness,
$a_g = \mle{\psia(\widehat w_g)}(r^*)$ for all $g$. The two
$2^{-\Kbits}$ terms come from \ref{chk:coherence} and \ref{chk:discharge}
respectively.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
We chain the four checks from the commitment inward. Throughout,
``honest value of $Z$'' means the value $Z$ would take for the committed
messages $\widehat w_g$ obtained by decoding.

\emph{(i) Proximity pins $\mathrm{cr}'$ (\ref{chk:encoding}).} The column-opening
test is the Brakedown proximity check on the $\gamma$- and $c$-folded
codeword. By the proximity lemma for the code, except with probability
$\epsilon_{\mathrm{prox}}(t,\delta)$ the committed columns
$\mat{C}_g[i,\cdot]$ are (correlated-)close to codewords; the extractor
decodes them to messages $\widehat w_g$, and the encoding identity at the
$t$ random positions forces
\[
  \mathrm{cr}' \;=\; \sum_g \gamma_g \sum_i c_i\,\widehat M_{w_g}[i,\cdot]
  \qquad\text{(the honest fold of the committed messages)},
\]
since two distinct messages whose encodings agree at $t$ random positions
violate the distance bound. Thus $\mathrm{cr}'$ is determined by
$(\widehat w_g, \gamma, c)$.

\emph{(ii) Coherence pins $b'$ (\ref{chk:coherence}).} Let
$\widehat b'[i] = \sum_g \gamma_g \sum_j q_1[j]\,\widehat M_{w_g}[i,j]$ be
the honest row-fold. By the completeness computation,
$\sum_j q_1[j]\,\mathrm{cr}'[j] = \sum_i c_i\,\widehat b'[i]$ with the
$\mathrm{cr}'$ pinned in (i). \ref{chk:coherence} therefore requires
$\sum_i c_i\,(b'[i]-\widehat b'[i]) = 0$ in $\Kpoly$. If
$b' \ne \widehat b'$, some coefficient slot $b$ has
$\bigl(b'[i]-\widehat b'[i]\bigr)_b$ nonzero for some $i$; as $c$ is drawn
\emph{after} $b'$ is absorbed, that slot's equation
$\sum_i c_i\,(b'[i]-\widehat b'[i])_b = 0$ is a nontrivial $\K$-linear
form in the uniform $c$, satisfied with probability $2^{-\Kbits}$. Hence
$b' = \widehat b'$ except with probability $2^{-\Kbits}$.

\emph{(iii) Evaluation consistency pins $a'$ (\ref{chk:eval}).} \ref{chk:eval} is
deterministic: $a' = \sum_i q_0[i]\,b'[i]$. With $b'=\widehat b'$ from
(ii), this forces $a' = \sum_i q_0[i]\,\widehat b'[i] = \sum_g \gamma_g\,
\widehat a_g'$, the $\gamma$-batch of the committed messages' bit-slice
folds.

\emph{(iv) Discharge pins the claims (\ref{chk:discharge}).} Let
$\widehat a_g = \mle{\psia(\widehat w_g)}(r^*)$ be the true evaluations of
the committed witness. By \Cref{lem:bitslice} applied to
$\widehat w_g$, $\psia(\widehat a_g') = \widehat a_g$, so with $a'$ from
(iii), $\psia(a') = \sum_g \gamma_g\,\widehat a_g$. \ref{chk:discharge} requires
$\psia(a') = \sum_g \gamma_g\,a_g$, hence
$\sum_g \gamma_g\,(a_g - \widehat a_g) = 0$ in $\K$. The claims $a_g$ are
fixed by the upstream transcript before $\gamma$ is drawn; if any
$a_g \ne \widehat a_g$ the difference vector is nonzero and the equation
is a nontrivial $\K$-linear form in the uniform $\gamma$, satisfied with
probability $2^{-\Kbits}$. Hence $a_g = \widehat a_g$ for all $g$ except
with probability $2^{-\Kbits}$.

A union bound over (i), (ii), (iv) gives the stated
$\epsilon_{\mathrm{open}}$. The extractor of (i) outputs the witness; (ii)--(iv)
certify the claims are its true evaluations.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Where the batching gain lives]
The single equation $\sum_g \gamma_g\,(a_g-\widehat a_g)=0$ in step (iv)
pins \emph{all} $K$ per-column claims at once, at the cost of one
$\K$-element of soundness ($2^{-\Kbits}$), independent of $K$. This is the
$\gamma$-batch: the algebraic proof carries one triple $(a',b',\mathrm{cr}')$
rather than $K$, and the soundness pays a single Schwartz--Zippel term.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Composition with the host PIOP]
Composed with the upstream reduction (error $\epsilon_{\mathrm{pre}}$),
the augmented argument has knowledge-soundness error
$\epsilon_{\mathrm{pre}} + \epsilon_{\mathrm{open}}$. With
$\Kbits = 128$, $\Dval = 32$, and $t = 987$ at rate $1/4$, the two
algebraic terms are $2\cdot 2^{-128}$, far below the proximity term; the
column-opening parameter $t$ dominates the opening's soundness budget, as
in the integer pipeline. Under the Fiat--Shamir/BCS transform in the
random-oracle model, the round-by-round structure of the four checks
(each challenge drawn after the data it tests is absorbed) yields a
non-interactive argument with the same error up to the standard ROM
losses.
\end{remark}
