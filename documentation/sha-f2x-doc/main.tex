\title{The SHA-256 $\F_2[X]$-AIR \\
       \large Binius-style arithmetisation over $\F_2[X]$}
\author{Albert Garreta \\[2pt] \normalsize Nethermind Research}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

\begin{center}
\textcolor{blue}{\textit{Draft companion to the all-$\Q[X]$ \texttt{sha-uair-doc}.
This document describes a different arithmetisation of the same SHA-256
relation, committing natively over $\F_2[X]$ and treating AND as a
column-level Hadamard-product relation whose proof mechanism is left
as a design parameter.}}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.5em}

\begin{abstract}
We describe a SHA-256 arithmetisation that commits all witness columns
as bit-polynomials in $\F_2[X]$ rather than lifting them to $\Q[X]$
with overflow witnesses. The design rests on four primitives:
\emph{(i)}~$\XOR$ is realised as polynomial addition in $\F_2[X]$ and
is free at the commitment layer; \emph{(ii)}~$\AND$ is realised as
coefficient-wise (Hadamard) multiplication, declared as a column-level
Hadamard-product relation whose discharge mechanism is deferred as a
design parameter; \emph{(iii)}~$\SHR$ and $\ROTR$ are realised as
bit-level linear maps on bit-polynomial columns with the access
mechanism for shifted values likewise deferred; \emph{(iv)}~modular
addition $\pmod{2^{32}}$ is realised by the \emph{per-step Binius
identity with virtual carry} in the quotient ring $\F_2[X]/(X^{32})$:
for each binary step $\bp{s} \equiv \bp{a} + \bp{b} \pmod{2^{32}}$,
the carry word $\bp{c} \in \bitpoly{32}$ has its lower $31$ bits
defined as $\SHR^{1}$ of the XOR difference $\bp{s} + \bp{a} + \bp{b}$
— a free combination of committed columns — and only the carry-out
of bit~$31$ (one $\F_2$ bit per row) is committed; the binary
addition is then pinned by the linear identity $\bp{s} = \bp{a} +
\bp{b} + X\cdot\bp{c}$ together with the Binius full-adder Hadamard
$(\bp{a} + X\cdot\bp{c}) \had (\bp{b} + X\cdot\bp{c}) = \bp{c} +
X\cdot\bp{c}$ in $\F_2[X]/(X^{32})$. Multi-input modular sums chain
$n - 1$ such binary steps with $n - 2$ intermediate-sum columns.
Multiplication by $X$ in $\F_2[X]/(X^{32})$ is the genuine ring
operation; an LSB-only public compensator absorbs the bit-$0$ residue
on inactive rows. We assemble these primitives into a complete
chained-compression AIR following the column- and constraint-layout
conventions of \texttt{sha-uair-doc}. The result is a constraint
system whose final state is a single MLE-evaluation claim per
committed column over the verifier's challenge extension field, with
tight local soundness on every modular addition (no appeal to the
global $H_N$ boundary check needed for individual adds).
\end{abstract}
